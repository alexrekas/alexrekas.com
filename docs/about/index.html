About

I help teams turn complex rules and legacy systems into products people can actually use.

I'm a Design Lead in Richmond, VA, with 12+ years designing digital products. Since 2020 I've led design at IdeaCrew on Enroll App, the platform behind the health insurance marketplaces for Washington, DC, Maine and Massachusetts, and I present designs directly to the states that run them. Before that, I designed enterprise software at Distributed Solutions and worked as a contract designer for clients including Marriott and the CDC.

I work embedded with engineers, from audits and research through prototypes and handoff, and I build accessibility in from the start. I prototype every day in Claude and Figma Make, and I direct AI coding agents to take designs from Figma into production-ready components, while I make the design calls and review every result.

I'm drawn to software where the rules are complicated and the stakes are real, where a confusing screen costs someone money, coverage or time. For the last few years that's been health insurance, but the same problems show up in fintech, B2B SaaS and workplace tools.

I do my best work on teams that treat design as part of building, not a coat of paint at the end: close to engineering, with direct access to the people making product decisions, and room to fix the cause of a problem instead of its symptoms.

Growing up, I was always taking things apart to understand why they were built the way they were. That curiosity led me to industrial design, where I fell in love with how people's stories shape the objects they use. It eventually pulled me into UX, where I do the same work on a screen.

Outside of work, you'll find me on the golf course or the soccer field, hunting for good coffee, or planning the next trip.
