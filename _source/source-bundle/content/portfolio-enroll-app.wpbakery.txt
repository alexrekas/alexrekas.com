[vc_row][vc_column][vc_column_text]<h2 style="margin-top:80px">New employer enrollment</h2><p>Small businesses signing up for coverage used to face one long form: personal details, employer details, office locations and notice preferences all on a single page, ending in a red Confirm button. I split it into three short steps (Personal Information, Business Information and Communication) with a progress rail that shows where you are and what's left. Each step asks about one thing, and Save &amp; Exit lets people stop to find a document and pick up where they left off next time they log in.</p>[/vc_column_text][/vc_column][/vc_row]

[vc_row][vc_column][vc_column_text]<h2 style="margin-top:80px">On a phone</h2><p>A significant share of marketplace users only have a phone, but the old app had no mobile support. Every step of the new flow was designed at phone width first, then expanded for desktop. Fields stack in a single column, actions stay at the bottom of the step, and nothing requires horizontal scrolling.</p>[/vc_column_text][/vc_column][/vc_row]

[vc_row][vc_column][vc_column_text]<h2 style="margin-top:80px">Accessible by default</h2><p>The audit found federal accessibility violations that made enrollment impossible for some people with disabilities. Rather than patch screens one at a time, I built WCAG AA and Section 508 requirements into the design system's foundations (color, type, components and interaction states), so every new feature inherits them. Every screen also carries a language switch (English, Español and Amharic) and a help line with TTY, and an “I don't have a SSN” option keeps people without one from getting stuck.</p>[/vc_column_text][/vc_column][/vc_row]
