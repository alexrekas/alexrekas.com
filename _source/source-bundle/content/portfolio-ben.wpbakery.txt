[vc_row][vc_column][vc_column_text]<h2 style="margin-top:80px">Adirondack comfort, De Stijl form</h2><p>A beach-vacation Adirondack chair started the idea; a college studio on the Netherlands gave it a style. De Stijl stripped form down to essentials and let parts appear to float. An outer frame steadies the bench, physically and visually, while the seat floats inside it. A later version drops the frame so it sits lower and steadier.</p>[/vc_column_text][/vc_column][/vc_row]
