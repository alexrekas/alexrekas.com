[vc_row][vc_column][vc_column_text]<h2 style="margin-top:80px">Daily check-in</h2><p>Every morning, employees answer two questions before coming in: have they been in close contact with someone with COVID-19 since their last screening, and do they have any of the listed symptoms. A summary lets them review and attest before submitting, and the result is unmistakable: OK to Enter, or Stop and stay home, with CDC resources and a self-isolation date worked out for them. Guests can screen without an account, and anyone not working that day can say so and pick a reason.</p>[/vc_column_text][/vc_column][/vc_row]

[vc_row][vc_column][vc_column_text]<h2 style="margin-top:80px">Management dashboard</h2><p>Managers needed two pictures at once: what was happening in the community and what was happening on their own team. The dashboard pairs public-health trends with the organization's screening results, and lists every employee's latest status with filters by location.</p><p>Early on, the CEO wanted the employee list as cards. I pushed for a table instead, because managers needed to compare a large set of employees quickly, and a table makes that easy. Cards might have looked nicer, but this was a call to put UX above UI, and the table shipped in the final design.</p>[/vc_column_text][/vc_column][/vc_row]

[vc_row][vc_column][vc_column_text]<h2 style="margin-top:80px">A brand for an anxious moment</h2><p>WorkRadar needed to feel calm and trustworthy at a time of real public anxiety. I created the name, logo, color palette and visual language from scratch, outside IdeaCrew's existing design system. The chevron in the wordmark points inward: a return to the workplace, to normalcy, to each other.</p>[/vc_column_text][/vc_column][/vc_row]
