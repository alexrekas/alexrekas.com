[vc_row][vc_column][vc_column_text]<h2 style="margin-top:80px">How I evaluated</h2><p>I reviewed each page against eight heuristics: consistency, standardization, cognitive load, navigational effort, aesthetic integrity, information architecture, recognition and linguistic clarity. Each finding got a low, moderate or high priority based on how much it hurt the experience.</p>[/vc_column_text][/vc_column][/vc_row]
