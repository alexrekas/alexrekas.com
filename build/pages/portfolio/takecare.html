The mark

The new identity trades a corporate look for a lifestyle brand's warmth, putting the weight on "Care." Its three dots stand for TakeCare's three pillars, Me, You+Me and We, and read as an ellipsis: more to come.

Color and type

A deep violet leads the palette, chosen for optimism and forward momentum, with yellow, magenta and teal for range and charcoal for text.

One system, many programs

The system extends to two tiers of sub-brands: Hero Programs that run year over year, and Regular Programs for recurring events. Each inherits TakeCare's language while keeping its own identity.

Imagery

Photography mixes stock and associate images to reflect Marriott's global workforce, art-directed to stay warm and uncluttered.

Brand book and templates

A brand book, digital templates and a curated image library let Marriott's teams make new materials without starting from scratch.
