New employer enrollment

Small businesses signing up for coverage used to face one long form: personal details, employer details, office locations and notice preferences all on a single page, ending in a red Confirm button. I split it into three short steps (Personal Information, Business Information and Communication) with a progress rail that shows where you are and what's left. Each step asks about one thing, and Save & Exit lets people stop to find a document and pick up where they left off next time they log in.

On a phone

A significant share of marketplace users only have a phone, but the old app had no mobile support. Every step of the new flow was designed at phone width first, then expanded for desktop. Fields stack in a single column, actions stay at the bottom of the step, and nothing requires horizontal scrolling.

Accessible by default

The audit found federal accessibility violations that made enrollment impossible for some people with disabilities. Rather than patch screens one at a time, I built WCAG AA and Section 508 requirements into the design system's foundations (color, type, components and interaction states), so every new feature inherits them. Every screen also carries a language switch (English, Español and Amharic) and a help line with TTY, and an “I don't have a SSN” option keeps people without one from getting stuck.
